Focus Reminder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

1. Privacy Policy

Focus Reminder does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

2. Data Collection

Focus Reminder does not collect personal information. The extension does not send user data to external servers and does not use analytics, tracking tools, or remote code.

3. Local Storage

All settings, monitored websites, timers, and usage statistics are stored locally in the user's browser using Chrome local storage.

4. Permissions

Focus Reminder only accesses user-selected websites in order to display focus reminders, manage optional session timers, and close the tab after the countdown ends.

5. Third Parties

No personal information is shared with third parties.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at your-email@example.com.
